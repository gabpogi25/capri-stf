# Photo credits

`sacre_coeur/` holds ten photos of the Sacré-Cœur, Paris, copied unchanged from the sample
data of hloc (Hierarchical-Localization, https://github.com/cvg/Hierarchical-Localization,
`datasets/sacre_coeur/mapping/`; repository licence Apache-2.0). The hloc README states that
these images come from the Image Matching Challenge 2021
(https://www.cs.ubc.ca/research/image-matching-challenge/2021/data/) and were originally
collected by the Yahoo Flickr Creative Commons 100M (YFCC100M) dataset, i.e. Flickr photos
published under Creative Commons licences by their authors. Check the licence of an
individual photo before reusing it outside this repository.

They are used only for the illustration in the graphical abstracts
(`experiments/run_abstract_photo_demo.py`), not for any evaluation.
